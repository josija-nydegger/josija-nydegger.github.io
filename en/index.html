Josija Nydegger

Civil Engineering · Bern University of Applied Sciences

I study civil engineering part-time at Bern University of Applied Sciences in Burgdorf, and alongside my studies I work as a working student in the infrastructure engineering department at BSB + Partner in Bern. In November 2026, I'm moving to structural engineering for buildings at Baukonstrukt in Biel. I'm particularly interested in structural engineering, digital construction and BIM (for example with IfcOpenShell), numerical simulation, and how custom software can make engineering work easier.

Before that, I studied civil engineering at ETH Zurich, along with a first year of computer science. My bachelor's thesis there dealt with the fluid-structure interaction of a wind turbine blade in COMSOL. After that, my path took me into the social sector for a while: first in an interim role in the office of the head of social services at the City of Opfikon, and then during my Swiss civilian service at a low-threshold drop-in and counseling center in the city of Zurich. I then returned to civil engineering.

In my free time, I build small tools, which you can find on GitHub, and I run anything from 10K to marathon distance.
